# TheBrief methodology

This is an operational synthesis of the information-style method in *Пиши, сокращай* and the editor's supplied study notes. It is not a substitute for the book and does not reproduce it.

## Editorial hierarchy

Work from meaning outward:

1. Reader and task: who reads, what they already know, and what changes after reading.
2. Claim and support: the conclusion, mechanism, facts, examples, limitations, and next step.
3. Structure: a sequence the reader can follow without reconstructing the author's thought.
4. Paragraphs and sentences: one local job at a time.
5. Words: remove friction only after the meaning is sound.

Do not polish weak meaning into confident prose. Expose the gap.

## Strong explanatory copy

### Orient the reader

Start with the result, practical tension, or familiar situation. State why the subject matters before asking the reader to absorb detail. For complex material, move from familiar experience to a new concept and then to the formal term.

### Show the mechanism

Answer the causal questions the reader would ask:

- What is it?
- Why does it exist?
- Where does the result or return come from?
- What changes it?
- What can go wrong?
- What does that mean for this reader?

Definitions are insufficient when the reader needs a decision. Follow a definition with a mechanism or scenario.

### Ground abstractions

Pair a general claim with something observable: a scenario, calculation, comparison, historical baseline, process, or consequence. Translate specialist numbers into a useful frame without distorting them.

Use examples to teach the rule, not decorate it. Keep a comparison on like-for-like terms: same period, risk, currency, base, and measurement method where relevant.

### Use honest evidence

Replace praise with proof. Keep attribution close to the claim. Distinguish fact, interpretation, forecast, and recommendation. Preserve degrees of confidence; `может`, `вероятно`, and a conditional are not interchangeable.

Name material limitations where they affect the conclusion. A caveat should help the reader weigh the claim, not merely protect the author.

## Language

Prefer actors and actions: `банк снизил ставку`, not `произошло снижение ставки`. Prefer the precise familiar word over a status-bearing synonym. Introduce a necessary term once in plain language, then use it consistently.

Remove or repair:

- introductory padding and verbal navigation that structure can replace;
- unproved evaluations and intensifiers;
- clichés, bureaucratic constructions, and fashionable abstractions;
- nominalizations hiding the actor or action;
- vague quantities and unattributed consensus;
- repeated conclusions and synonym chains;
- sentences carrying several new logical moves.

Deletion is not the finish line. Replace empty claims with useful content when the brief supports it.

## Structure

Make the first sentences of paragraphs tell the story on their own. Each paragraph advances one topic; each heading names what the section gives the reader. Put prerequisites before conclusions that rely on them.

Use lists for real parallel sets, choices, steps, or checks. Prose is better when the relationship is causal or argumentative.

Headlines state the subject and useful conclusion without intrigue that withholds the point. Commercial copy may lead with benefit, provided the text proves it and states material restrictions.

## Naturalness check

Read the result as speech. Repair abrupt compression, monotonous sentence patterns, fake friendliness, excessive headings, and mechanical removal of every connective. The final text should feel written by a competent person for another competent person.

## Final editorial check

- The reader, task, and intended action are identifiable.
- The main claim appears early and is supported.
- Necessary terms are explained without condescension.
- Facts, interpretations, and forecasts are distinguishable.
- Numbers have a base, period, comparison, or consequence when needed.
- Risks and boundaries remain visible.
- Paragraphs form a causal sequence rather than a data dump.
- No important meaning disappeared during compression.
- The language sounds natural in Russian.
