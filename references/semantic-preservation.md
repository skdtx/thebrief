# Semantic preservation

Use this reference whenever editing supplied copy.

## Meaning contract

Privately record:

- **subject:** what the text is about;
- **purpose:** why it exists;
- **audience:** whose decision or understanding matters;
- **action:** what the reader should do or be ready to do;
- **claims:** facts, interpretations, promises, and recommendations;
- **stance:** favorable, neutral, cautious, critical, or mixed;
- **confidence:** certainty, probability, scenario, or open question;
- **boundaries:** risks, exclusions, conditions, approvals, and legal wording.

The edited version passes only if these remain materially equivalent.

## Safe editorial freedom

Apply without approval when meaning remains equivalent:

- reorder material into a clearer causal sequence;
- split or combine sentences and paragraphs;
- replace jargon with a plain explanation while retaining the term if needed;
- remove genuine repetition and unsupported decoration;
- add headings, labels, or navigation;
- surface an existing conclusion earlier;
- convert passive or nominal language into actors and actions.

## Author decision boundary

Do not silently apply a change that:

- adds, removes, strengthens, or weakens a claim;
- changes who the text addresses;
- changes the desired action or sales objective;
- turns information into advice or a forecast into a promise;
- removes a risk, condition, exception, or attribution;
- invents a benefit, proof point, number, source, or product term;
- resolves a contradiction by choosing one side;
- changes a neutral explanation into promotion or vice versa.

Place it under `Требует решения автора` with the current issue, proposed wording, and meaning consequence.

## Missing and doubtful information

Use:

- `[Нужен факт: какой факт и для какого утверждения]`
- `[Гипотеза для проверки: возможное содержание]`

A hypothesis is drafting help, not evidence. Keep it outside clean final copy unless the user explicitly wants marked placeholders inside the draft.

## Post-edit comparison

Compare original and result on every meaning-contract field. Pay special attention to modals, negation, quantities, time periods, attribution, comparison bases, eligibility, fees, guarantees, and calls to action. Report any unresolved mismatch instead of declaring the edit complete.
