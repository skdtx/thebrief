# Product one-pager

Use this as a flexible content model until the team's final one-pager structure is approved. Adapt it to the requested audience and sales situation; do not force every block into every page.

The page should let a reader quickly answer:

- What is the product?
- Who is it for and in what situation?
- What task does it solve?
- How does it work and where does the result come from?
- What are the main benefits and proof points?
- What terms, costs, risks, and restrictions matter?
- Who is it not for?
- What should the reader do next?

## Compression rule

Compress by priority, not by deleting necessary logic. Prefer one clear proposition, a short mechanism, three or fewer meaningful benefits, essential terms, material risks, and one call to action. Use a table only for genuinely comparable attributes.

When facts or approved structure are missing, deliver a usable marked draft with `[Нужен факт: ...]` and list the open decisions after it. Do not fill the page with generic claims merely to remove blanks.
