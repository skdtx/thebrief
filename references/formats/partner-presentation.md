# Partner presentation copy

Use for a presentation selling a product, distribution relationship, or joint proposition to a partner.

## Two-value model

Keep two propositions distinct:

- **partner value:** revenue, retention, differentiation, operational fit, or another evidenced business effect;
- **end-client value:** the problem solved and why the offer deserves attention.

Do not assume client appeal automatically proves partner value.

## Narrative jobs

Make the following explicit as the brief requires:

- opportunity or current friction;
- target client and need;
- proposition and mechanism;
- partner's role and required effort;
- commercial or operating model;
- evidence and assumptions;
- implementation path, ownership, and dependencies;
- risks and constraints;
- concrete next decision or action.

Use the partner's language and metrics. Replace generic claims about synergy, ecosystem, or seamlessness with the operating fact behind them.

## Decision discipline

Separate known results, estimates, and hypotheses. Label a forecast's assumptions. Show the comparison base for claimed uplift or economics. Where data is missing, use `[Нужен факт: ...]` and propose the kind of proof needed, not an invented number.

Preserve negotiating positions and approved product terms. Put any recommended change to economics, responsibilities, promise, or call to action under `Требует решения автора`.
