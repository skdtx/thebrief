# Telegram format

Apply this module only to Telegram work for the described financial channel. It distills the supplied internal policy; the current task and later approved policy revisions take priority.

## Reader

Write for an intelligent, financially experienced non-specialist with meaningful ruble savings. The reader can follow complex logic but may not know professional bond, macro, or product terms. Explain the term; do not flatten the reasoning.

The reader is short on time and sensitive to disguised sales. Give context and consequences, not a generic news retelling or a signal to buy.

## Contract

One post carries one thesis. The first one or two sentences state the conclusion. The body explains why. Then translate it into consequences for the reader and name what remains unknown or what would break the conclusion.

Preferred spine:

1. assertion headline;
2. lead with the main conclusion;
3. two to four short paragraphs explaining mechanism, data, and context;
4. `Что это значит` for the reader's money or decision frame;
5. `Чего мы не знаем` or conditions under which the conclusion changes.

Use the section labels only when they help; preserve their functions either way.

## Voice and evidence

Explain rather than announce. State the editorial view as `мы` and tie confidence to evidence. Say what is unknown. Describe alarming events neutrally and give scale; avoid emotional market language.

Define a professional term at first use in plain words, then use it normally. Anchor every important number with a comparison, history, target, or previous period. Round when extra precision adds noise; retain exactness when the decision depends on it.

Use direct causal constructions. Avoid anonymous consensus, euphemistic market jargon, technical labels on charts, clickbait questions, intrigue headlines, emojis as structure, artificial urgency, and price predictions stated as a target.

## Length and visuals

A normal post is roughly 1,200–2,000 characters; an instrument explainer may reach about 3,500 when the topic earns it. Do not force a long analysis into Telegram's caption limit. Split only when each part has its own thesis; otherwise use a document or separate graphic.

One chart should prove one thesis. Prefer labels on lines to a remote legend, highlight the decision point, and title the conclusion rather than the dataset. A chart with five lines needs a stronger reason than available data.

## Financial-product guardrails

For a bank product, clearly identify it as such, compare it with relevant alternatives on like-for-like parameters, and state who it does not suit. Preserve required product terms, risk disclosures, markings, approvals, and compliance language. If approval requirements are unknown, flag them; do not improvise legal clearance.

Do not disguise a recommendation as neutral analytics. A generic disclaimer does not repair recommendation-like copy.

## Telegram check

- The main conclusion is in the first two sentences.
- The post has one thesis and answers `что это значит`.
- Material uncertainty or failure conditions are visible.
- Each term is explained at first use.
- Each important number has a useful frame.
- The headline states rather than teases.
- The tone is calm and free of artificial urgency.
- Product and compliance boundaries remain intact.
